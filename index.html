Our Promise To You

I divide all readers into two classes; those who read to remember and those who read to forget! I have always imagined that Paradise will be a kind of library. I have traveled many lands, met many people, and in all those places, the greatest joy I have known is to lose myself in a book, to read for hours without noticing time pass, to enter worlds that are not my own and yet feel entirely familiar, to encounter ideas, feelings, and lives that stretch beyond my own limited experience. A library, a book, is not just paper and ink, it is the door to infinite lives, the keys to understanding humanity, the mirror of our own soul, and the seed of our imagination. In reading, I live a thousand lives, and each page turns into a path leading to a new discovery, a new wonder, a new understanding of myself and the world.
